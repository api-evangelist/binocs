# Binocs

> Agentic AI-powered due diligence and strategic advisory platform for financial professionals — private equity, consulting, corporates and portfolio companies spanning private credit, venture capital and venture debt. Delivers decision-ready diligence in minutes at a fraction of traditional consulting cost.

Binocs is a SaaS application (app.binocs.co). It does not currently publish a public developer API, SDKs, or developer documentation. The links below are the public product, legal, and security surfaces.
